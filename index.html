HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

  • WASD Move
  • Mouse Aim · LMB Fire
  • RMB Aim down sights / scope
  • Shift Walk (silent, accurate)
  • Ctrl Crouch · Space Jump
  • R Reload · Q Last weapon
  • 1–5 / Wheel / V Switch weapon
  • X ☀ SUNBURST ultimate (charge it with kills)
  • Tab Match stats · Esc Pause
  • Hands-free: 😮 fire · 🙂 smile = switch weapon · 😗 kiss = SUNBURST · 🤨 brows = reload

THE MISSION

  • Clear 13 rounds of Spectre bots. Each round sends a bigger, sharper squad.
  • Headshots deal heavy bonus damage. Aim head-height at corners.
  • Standing still or walking makes your first shots laser accurate — stop, then shoot.
  • Grab medkits and ammo crates around the arena; they respawn.
  • Rounds cleared fast with high accuracy raise your end rank — chase the S.
  • The OGRE rocket launcher hits everything near the blast — including you. Jump with it if you dare.
  • Kills fill the ☀ SUNBURST meter. When it blazes, press X to call an orbital strike where you're aiming — bots under roofs survive it.
